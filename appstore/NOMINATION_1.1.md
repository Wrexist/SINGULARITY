## Description (≤1000 characters)

Singularity Inc. is a satirical idle tycoon about the AI gold rush. It's built by a solo developer and has no ads, no energy timers and no pay-to-win. Version 1.1 is our biggest update since launch. It turns a GPU clicker into a full lab-management game: hire a team with roles and morale, launch AI products and grow their revenue, and kit out racks in the new Rig Bay. Every prestige ("Ship the Model") is now a real decision. Players declare a Stance (Safety, Center or Acceleration), draft one of three Lab Charters that can rewrite the rules, and can take on Unplugged Trials, a full run with their permanent bonuses switched off. The 2.5D data center physically grows as you play. The game runs fully offline and collects no data. 1.1 also brings full VoiceOver and keyboard support, Reduce Motion, 44pt tap targets and smoother performance on older iPhones.
