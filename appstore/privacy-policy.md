# Privacy Policy — Singularity Inc.
*Last updated: 2026-10-11*

Singularity Inc. ("the app") is a single-player, offline game. We designed it to collect as little as possible: no personal data, and nothing used for advertising
or tracking.

## What we collect
**No personal data is collected.** The app has no accounts, no login, no advertising, and no
tracking. The only data that leaves your device is your purchase history, described under Purchases.

## Data stored on your device
Your game progress and settings are saved **locally on your device only** (standard on-device
storage). We never see this data. Deleting the app removes it.

## Purchases
Optional purchases (Singularity Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock) are processed by **Apple**
via the App Store. We never receive your payment information.

To check which purchases you own, the app uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy), a
subscription service. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase history, tied to a random ID created
on your device. It does not receive your name, email or Apple ID. It is used only to unlock Pro and to
give us totals such as how many people subscribe, never for advertising or tracking.

## Children
The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. There is no chat, no user-generated
content, and no external links to unvetted content.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
