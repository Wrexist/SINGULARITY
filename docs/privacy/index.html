Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Singularity Inc. (“the app”) is a single-player, offline game. We designed it to collect as little as possible: no personal data, and nothing used for advertising or tracking.

What we collect

No personal data is collected. The app has no accounts, no login, no advertising, and no tracking. The only data that leaves your device is your purchase history, described under Purchases below.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. We never see this data. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Optional purchases (Singularity Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime unlock) are processed by Apple via the App Store. We never receive your payment information.

To check which purchases you own, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase history, tied to a random ID created on your device. It does not receive your name, email or Apple ID. It is used only to unlock Pro and to give us totals such as how many people subscribe, never for advertising or tracking.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. There is no chat, no user-generated content, and no links to unvetted external content.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
